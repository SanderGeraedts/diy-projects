# Decisions

Read the global decisions first (`node_modules/@sandergeraedts/harness/decisions/index.md`),
then this list, then only the records your work touches. How the registry works is in
[README.md](README.md). One line per record: `- [D-NNNN Title](file.md): one sentence`.

## Accepted

### Process

- [D-0030 This project follows the harness](D-0030-this-project-follows-the-harness.md): The harness is installed as a pinned dev dependency; its rules and global decisions (G-NNNN) apply here unless a project decision overrules one, and the project decisions whose substance is now global are replaced by them.
- [D-0010 The repo is SanderGeraedts/diy-projects, private until v1 works, then public under MIT](D-0010-private-personal-repo-mit-later.md): The code lives on Sander's personal GitHub account as diy-projects, private while it's rough; once v1 works and the deploy docs are generic it goes public under the MIT licence.
- [D-0036 The laptop keeps its own TrueNAS key as a fallback for when the runner on the NAS is unavailable; harness nas --remote stays the normal route](D-0036-the-laptop-keeps-its-own-truenas-key-as-a-fallback.md): Overrules G-0026 for one key: Sander's laptop keeps the claude-agent TrueNAS API key in his Windows user environment, used with harness nas run locally on the home network only when harness nas --remote can't run; agents never read, print or carry it, and every other rule of G-0026 and G-0027 holds.
- [D-0043 A check of what a user sees reads the outcome in every state the app passes through, and a regression check fails on a fresh build of main](D-0043-checks-of-what-a-user-sees-read-the-outcome-in-every-state.md): A Done when or test for anything a user sees checks what is actually rendered or delivered, not an attribute that should lead to it, on first load, after background work changes the data, after navigating away and back, after a reload and at the device extremes (including pixel ratio 1); a check meant to fail before the fix is shown failing on a fresh build of main. Proposed to the harness as G-0033, which replaces this record when it is released.
- [D-0044 Netlify skips the deploy preview and the production build of a change that touches only documentation](D-0044-netlify-skips-builds-of-docs-only-changes.md): Overrules G-0018's deploy preview for every pull request, for docs-only changes: netlify.toml's [build] ignore runs a script that skips the Netlify build (preview and production) when every changed path is under docs/, a Markdown file at the root, or a Markdown file under deploy/ or .claude/; anything else, any doubt and any error builds.

### Architecture

- [D-0002 The app is an offline-first PWA; PocketBase on the NAS is the source of truth](D-0002-offline-first-pwa-with-the-nas-as-source-of-truth.md): Each device keeps a full local copy plus an outbox of changes and works with no connection; PocketBase on the NAS is the master, photos are append-only, and text edits are last-write-wins with the overwritten version kept in history.
- [D-0007 The stack is a React + Vite SPA with TanStack Router, vite-plugin-pwa and Dexie, against PocketBase](D-0007-react-vite-spa-with-dexie-and-pocketbase.md): A static single-page app in React and TypeScript, built with Vite, routed with TanStack Router, made installable and offline with vite-plugin-pwa, storing records and photos in IndexedDB through Dexie; PocketBase is the backend.
- [D-0013 Sander signs in with email and password; a sign-in lasts 90 days and never blocks offline use; signing out clears the device](D-0013-sign-in-with-email-and-password-never-blocking-offline-use.md): One PocketBase user with email and password, a 90-day token renewed on every sync, an expired sign-in only pauses sync, and sign-out syncs, warns about anything unsynced, then clears the device's local copy.
- [D-0023 The front end is on Netlify at projects.sanderg.nl; the browser talks directly to PocketBase on the NAS through Tailscale Funnel; only the owner writes, anyone may read public projects](D-0023-backend-on-the-nas-with-owner-writes-and-public-reads.md): D-0005's hosting and lock-down, with one change: records stay owner-only to write, but public projects, their entries and their photo records, copies and thumbnails are readable without signing in; originals, private projects and history stay owner-only.
- [D-0026 A permanent delete empties the records on the NAS and keeps each as a purge marker; a restore brings back what one delete cascaded to](D-0026-permanent-delete-leaves-a-purge-marker.md): Adds to D-0014's bin: a restore clears the tombstones that share the item's deletedAt; a permanent delete is the owner's DELETE of a tombstoned project or entry, taken over by a PocketBase hook that empties it and everything under it, drops their files and history, and keeps the record with purgedAt so late writes get 410 and other devices drop their copies.
- [D-0038 Thumbnails are 1280 px on the long edge; cards and day grids show the thumbnail, with the copy as a srcset candidate](D-0038-thumbnails-are-1280-px-and-grids-pick-by-screen.md): Adds to D-0003 and D-0015: a photo's thumbnail is 1280 px on the long edge (half the 2560 px copy) instead of about 512; the overview's cards and the project page's tiles, the owner's and a visitor's, show it through srcset and sizes with the copy as the larger candidate; downscales of more than 2x are done in halving steps; each device rebuilds its own older thumbnails from its copy.
- [D-0039 The owner's devices upload each rebuilt 1280 px thumbnail once, over the older one on the NAS](D-0039-rebuilt-thumbnails-replace-the-older-ones-on-the-nas.md): Answers Q-007 and adds to D-0002 and D-0038: a photo's thumbnail on the NAS may be replaced (the only file of a photo that may change after it was added); an owner device uploads its rebuilt thumbnail through the normal sync, only while the NAS's thumbnail is still narrower, without a new updatedAt, history or unsent count, so visitors see older photos sharp too.
- [D-0040 A thumbnail this device hasn't measured is offered alone, and a shown photo keeps its addresses while its files are the same](D-0040-unmeasured-thumbnails-are-offered-alone-and-photo-addresses-stay-put.md): Adds to D-0038: on cards and tiles a stored photo whose thumbnail width isn't measured yet is shown from its thumbnail only, without the copy as a srcset candidate; and the object URLs of a stored photo stay the same while its copy and thumbnail are the same files, so a write to photos doesn't reload every card.
- [D-0041 Cards and day tiles also offer a 640×480 card image: each device makes its own from its copy and keeps it, and PocketBase makes it from the thumbnail for visitors](D-0041-cards-and-tiles-also-offer-a-640-px-card-image.md): Adds to D-0038 and D-0040, and replaces D-0015 to allow one PocketBase thumb size: the overview's cards and the project page's tiles get a third srcset candidate, a 640×480 centre crop (the cards' and tiles' 4:3 crop at a pixel ratio of 2); the owner's devices make it locally from the copy and never sync it; a visitor gets it from PocketBase's ?thumb=640x480 on the photo's thumbnail; everything else of D-0015 holds.
- [D-0042 A card's or tile's srcset uses addresses of its own, never one another image or an earlier candidate set has loaded](D-0042-each-srcset-place-gets-its-own-photo-addresses.md): Adds to D-0040 and D-0041: Chrome takes a larger srcset candidate whose address it has already loaded in the page, so the thumbnail and copy addresses in a card's or tile's srcset are kept per place and candidate set, apart from the viewer's and from the addresses before the card image arrived; for visitors the same through a marker in the query.

### Product

- [D-0003 Photos are added from the gallery, grouped by date taken, kept offline at 2560 px, with originals only on the NAS](D-0003-photos-come-from-the-gallery-with-a-phone-sized-copy-offline.md): Photos taken in the Pixel camera app are added later through a multi-select picker and grouped into entries by EXIF date taken; the phone keeps every photo as a 2560 px WebP/AVIF copy and the untouched original lives only on the NAS.
- [D-0014 Deleting a project or entry is a soft delete that cascades to its photos; a bin to restore or permanently delete comes after v1](D-0014-deleting-is-a-soft-delete-that-cascades-a-bin-comes-after-v1.md): In v1, deleting an entry tombstones it and its photos, and deleting a project tombstones it, its entries and their photos, after a confirmation naming the photo count; everything stays on the NAS. After v1, a bin lets Sander restore or permanently delete.
- [D-0020 The app's look follows sanderg.nl: Poppins, a pink/orange accent with text-width bars, the system theme, Dutch, built with Tailwind](D-0020-design-system-with-text-width-accent-bars.md): D-0011's design system with one change: every accent bar (header title, page titles, entry dates) is as wide as the text it underlines, with square ends, instead of a short rounded fixed-width bar.
- [D-0025 v1 is projects with status, tags and a public switch that is on by default, dated photo entries, offline search, and a read-only public view](D-0025-public-switch-on-by-default.md): D-0022's scope with one change: the public switch is on by default, for new projects and for the projects that exist when it ships; Sander switches off what he wants private.
- [D-0031 Addresses use a project slug, an entry date and a photo place, never an id; a day's photos are shown and numbered newest first](D-0031-photos-within-a-day-are-shown-newest-first.md): D-0029 with one change: an entry's photos are shown newest first (time taken, then when added, then id, each descending), and a photo's place in its address follows that order, so place 1 is the newest photo of the day.
- [D-0032 The overview lists projects by their newest photo, by time taken; projects without photos come last](D-0032-the-overview-lists-projects-by-their-newest-photo.md): Projects on the overview, the owner's and a visitor's, are ordered by the latest time taken among their live photos, newest first; ties by last edit, then id; projects without a live photo come after all others, by last edit.
- [D-0033 Photos can also be added by dropping them onto the owner's project page; they take the same import as the picker](D-0033-photos-can-be-dropped-onto-a-project-page.md): Adds to D-0003: image files dropped anywhere on the owner's project page go through the same import as "Foto's toevoegen" and land in the day they were taken; a drop never opens the file in the browser, anywhere in the app.
- [D-0035 A zip file dropped onto a project page is read on the device by its central directory, inflated with fflate, and its images take the normal import](D-0035-zip-files-are-read-by-their-central-directory-and-inflated-with-fflate.md): D-0034 with one change: the app finds a zip's entries itself from its central directory (and ZIP64), reads each image from its own slice of the file and checks its size and CRC-32; fflate only inflates. Lazy loading, precache, one image at a time, images only and the 40 MB limit stay.
- [D-0037 The photo viewer moves through all of a project's photos in the timeline's order, across days](D-0037-the-viewer-moves-through-the-whole-timeline.md): Arrow keys, the ‹ › buttons and swipes all go from a day's last photo to the first photo of the next day down the project page (and back), so "next" is always an older photo; days without live photos are skipped, for the owner and visitors; the counter and label stay per day, and a delete shows what next would have shown.

## Open questions

## Resolved questions

- [Q-001 How should the guarded-path review be enforced while the repo is private on a free plan?](Q-001-how-to-enforce-review-of-guarded-prs-on-a-private-free-repo.md): GitHub Free offers no branch protection, rulesets or code-owner reviews on private repos, so nothing on GitHub stops anyone with write access from merging a guarded PR; decide whether to accept that until the repo goes public, pay for Pro, or add a local guard.
- [Q-002 Can projects and entries be deleted, and what happens to their photos?](Q-002-can-projects-and-entries-be-deleted.md): D-0004 doesn't say whether projects and entries can be deleted and D-0002 only defines tombstones for photos; decide whether deleting is allowed, whether it cascades to photos, and whether anything is ever purged from the NAS.
- [Q-003 How does Sander sign in to PocketBase, and what happens when the sign-in expires offline?](Q-003-how-does-sander-sign-in.md): Sync needs a PocketBase sign-in (D-0005: sign-up closed, owner-only records); decide the sign-in method, how long a sign-in lasts, and that an expired sign-in never blocks offline use.
- [Q-004 How does a second device (the laptop, or a reinstalled phone) get the 2560 px copy of each photo?](Q-004-how-do-other-devices-get-the-phone-sized-copy.md): D-0003 has the phone make a 2560 px copy before sync and keeps only originals on the NAS; decide how any other device gets its offline copy without downloading every original.
- [Q-005 How are PocketBase's data and the photo originals on the NAS backed up?](Q-005-how-is-the-nas-data-backed-up.md): The NAS holds the only copy of every photo original (D-0003) and is the source of truth (D-0002), but no decision covers backups; decide what is backed up, where to, how often, and how a restore is tested.
- [Q-006 Does the laptop keep its own TrueNAS key now that NAS work runs through the runner on the NAS?](Q-006-does-the-laptop-keep-its-own-truenas-key.md): G-0026 holds the TrueNAS key only in the infrastructure repository's GitHub environment, but this project's NAS setup still has a key for the claude-agent user in Sander's user environment; decide whether to revoke it and work only through harness nas --remote, or keep it and overrule G-0026 here.
- [Q-007 Do the rebuilt 1280 px thumbnails replace the older 512 px ones on the NAS?](Q-007-do-rebuilt-thumbnails-replace-the-older-ones-on-the-nas.md): D-0038 makes thumbnails 1280 px and has each device rebuild its own older ones locally; decide whether the owner's device also uploads them over the 512 px thumbnails of existing photos on the NAS, so visitors see older photos sharp too.
